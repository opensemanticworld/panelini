# Getting started

Welcome! This section gets you from zero to a running panelini app in under five minutes.

## What panelini is

panelini is a thin, opinionated layout on top of [HoloViz Panel](https://panel.holoviz.org/). You compose your dashboard from Panel objects; panelini handles the responsive shell, theming, and the wiring for reusable panels (JSON forms, network graphs, LLM chat).

## Prerequisites

- **Python ≥ 3.11**
- A terminal and a browser
- Familiarity with [Panel](https://panel.holoviz.org/) helps but isn't required
